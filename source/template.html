    Where it stops

    A fit, not a proof

    • An illustration, not a tested course. No learning outcome has been measured. The games are real and each stretch matches the costs of the reply piece by piece; that shows the code can be read, not that chess and help threads work alike.
    • The 37 replies were chosen for what they teach, not drawn as a sample. The counts, the colour bars and the percentages describe this set, not the group or the forums.
    • One expert, one coder. The puzzles come from one expert in one group (57 first replies), coded by one author. That expert also develops QSWATMOD, the tool many of the questions are about, so some replies (use QSWATMOD, a fixed release) are a developer’s. In the comparison, SWAT-MODFLOW is that one expert, while SWAT, MODFLOW and APEX each pool several responders, so a difference can be a difference between people and not between communities.
    • The words are partly the author’s. The three levels were assigned by the author and are exploratory; the manual, the technician and the specialist are an analogy. The takeaways and the definitions of the families are the author’s own wording; the author has worked with SWAT-MODFLOW models.
    • No engine checks the moves. A puzzle accepts the move the grandmaster played, and another move can be as good. A move with no cost of its own (manner, the promise to look) leaves its piece free.
    • Some direct replies are shown mid-game. A resolved reply should end in a resignation; when no game ends in the right way, the stretch is taken from the middle of a game: the case is resolved, but nobody resigns.

    Games

    EventWhereWhenPuzzles
    Games: official FIDE event broadcasts as published on Lichess; players, ratings, venue and dates are those of the game records, and each rating is the one on the date of that game. Replies: first replies of the SWAT-MODFLOW group owner, paraphrased; the group owner is a co-author of this project. Puzzle progress stays in your browser. This page has no relation to FIDE, Lichess or the players, and nothing in it is a judgement on how anyone played. It is an interpretation of the study, not a result.
